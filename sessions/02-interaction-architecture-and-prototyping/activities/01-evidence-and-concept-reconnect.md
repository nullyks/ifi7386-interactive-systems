# Activity 01 - Evidence and Concept Reconnect

[← Session 2](../README.md) · [Glossary](../../../resources/glossary.md) · [Academic readings](../../../resources/academic-readings.md)

**Time:** 10 minutes · **Format:** team review and decision

## Goal

Bring forward the strongest evidence and the largest uncertainty from Session 1 so that today's models address the current concept rather than an outdated version.

## Inputs

Open your latest:

- user-context-problem statement;
- value proposition;
- six-step main scenario;
- low-fidelity prototype image;
- contextual observation or conversation notes;
- three assumptions or risks.

## Steps

1. **Evidence round - 3 minutes.** Each member names one observation or statement, not an interpretation.
2. **Change check - 3 minutes.** Mark what the evidence supports, weakens, or leaves unresolved.
3. **Select the focus - 2 minutes.** Choose one uncertain interaction or system behaviour that architecture and prototyping can make clearer today.
4. **Record the handover - 2 minutes.** Write the current concept and focus using the template below.

## Handover template

> Our primary user is **[role]** in **[context]**. The user needs to **[goal]**. Our current concept responds by **[main interaction]**. Evidence from **[source or situation]** suggests **[observation]**. We still need to make **[uncertain interaction or system behaviour]** clear enough to inspect and prototype.

## Evidence discipline

Keep these statements separate:

| Type | Example |
|---|---|
| Observation or report | A student said the group often notices discomfort only after someone interrupts the work. |
| Interpretation | Shared responsibility may delay action. |
| Design decision | The status cue should identify a possible next action without assigning responsibility to one person. |
| Remaining question | Will the cue support discussion, or will it create an unwanted interruption? |

## Output

One current concept handover and one focus for today's modelling and prototype work.
