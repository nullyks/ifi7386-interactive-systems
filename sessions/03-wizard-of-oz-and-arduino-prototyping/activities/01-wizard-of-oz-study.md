# Activity 01 - Wizard-of-Oz Study

[← Session 3](../README.md) · [Glossary](../../../resources/glossary.md)

**Time:** 35 minutes · **Format:** project-team study with a peer participant

## Goal

Use the prepared prototype and operator script to learn how a participant interprets the interaction before technical implementation begins.

## Part 1 - Set up (5 minutes)

Assign facilitator, wizard, observer, and participant roles. If your group is small, pair with another team. Place the operator where they can follow the script without cueing the participant. Check the two prepared task scenarios, permitted responses, timing, exception rules, and evidence sheet. Explain participation and any recording, and obtain consent.

## Part 2 - Run the task (15 minutes)

1. Give the participant the situation and goal; do not describe the controls or desired sequence.
2. Run the main task, then the meaningful alternative task if time permits.
3. The wizard follows the written triggers, rules, and response times. Mark any deviation.
4. The observer records actions, words, hesitation, feedback noticed, recovery attempts, and timestamps where timing matters.
5. Ask what the participant thought the system was doing and what they expected next.
6. Debrief which responses were simulated. Thank the participant and confirm how notes or recordings will be used.

## Part 3 - Interpret the evidence (15 minutes)

Record at least three rows. Keep the columns distinct.

| Observed action or words | Team interpretation | Design implication or next question |
|---|---|---|
|  |  |  |

Identify one result that affects a state, label, cue, timing, recovery path, or technical requirement. Note whether the study actually addressed the research question. Preserve an uncertainty if the evidence does not resolve it.

## Output

One study record with the prototype version, participant task, wizard-script deviations, at least three observations, one supported design implication, and one remaining question.
